6G Stainless Pipe Root Pass - Penetration Inconsistencies

Operative_77 verified

Running a 6G schedule 40 stainless (316L). Using a 3/32" tungsten, #8 cup, around 90 amps. I'm getting inconsistent root penetration on the bottom half (6 o'clock to 3 o'clock). Top half is laying in perfectly flat and tied in, but fighting gravity on the bottom is causing some suck-back.

Purge is running at 30 CFH. Gap is a tight 1/8", land is feathered to a knife edge. Am I running too hot on the bottom, or is my rod angle (feed) causing the chill?

> "Am I running too hot on the bottom, or is my rod angle (feed) causing the chill?"

It's a combination of both, but primarily your wire feed technique at 6 o'clock. When you're pushing up from the bottom, gravity pulls the puddle. If your gap is 1/8" and knife-edged, 90 amps is slightly hot unless your travel speed is perfectly calibrated.

Try this parameter adjustment: Drop to 82-85 amps. More importantly, don't just lay the wire in the gap. You need to employ a continuous feed, keeping the wire firmly pressed into the leading edge of the puddle to cool it instantly as it bridges the gap.
